Independent Electrical Services Ltd has been wiring homes, businesses and industrial sites across the NWT since 1986 — still run by the same family today.

Our story

Started in Yellowknife, still independent

Independent Electrical Services Ltd was founded in 1986 and has operated continuously in Yellowknife ever since, taking on residential, commercial and industrial electrical work — including contracts with government and commercial clients across the territory.

The company is owned and managed by Shirley Coumont, who brings more than 30 years of hands-on administrative and business experience to the company, alongside her son Kyle Coumont, a licensed electrician with over 10 years in the trade. Certified electricians and apprentices round out the crew.

In 1994, Shirley travelled to Ottawa to accept the Young Entrepreneur Award of the Year — recognition of the business she'd built from the ground up in Yellowknife.
