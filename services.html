Electrical services for homes, business & industry

From a single outlet to a full service panel upgrade, our licensed electricians handle it — with 24/7 emergency response when things can't wait.

Electrical Repairs & Maintenance

Troubleshooting, faulty wiring, flickering lights, tripped breakers — diagnosed and fixed for residential, commercial and industrial sites.

Generator Installation

Backup generator sizing and installation so winter outages don't take down your home or business.

Service Panel Upgrades

Upgrade an aging or undersized panel to safely support modern loads — done to code, properly permitted and inspected.

Solar Panel Installation

Surge Protection
